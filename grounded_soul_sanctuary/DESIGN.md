## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the ethos of grounded luxury, radical honesty, and soulful transformation. Crafted for holistic therapy centered on Shadow and Light Integration, it strictly avoids toxic positivity and airy spiritual bypassing. The visual environment serves as a sacred, grounded container: quiet, deeply safe, mature, and unhurried. 

The aesthetic is an intentional synthesis of **Warm Organic Editorial** and **Subtle Tactile Minimalism**. We employ serene, mineral tones and expansive negative space to induce nervous-system regulation. Surfaces mimic fine tactile cardstock rather than synthetic digital glass. Typography bridges classical literary contemplation with clear humanistic clarity, inviting the seeker to rest, reflect, and confront the full spectrum of their internal landscape.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from earth, botanical moss, weathered terracotta, and natural parchment. It balances cool botanical stability with human flesh tones and mineral resonance.

- **Primary (`#748873` - Sage Green):** The anchor of emotional safety and integration. Used for high-level editorial headers, primary framing, borders, and inverted deep-immersion spaces.
- **Secondary / Accent (`#A77F60` - Earthy Bronze):** The catalyst for intentional action. Reserved for interactive controls, focused states, primary calls-to-action, meaningful underlines, and glyph accents.
- **Tertiary (`#FFDCDC` - Pale Pink):** The tender inner child and vulnerable shadow. Used sparingly for soft highlights, contemplation cards, retreat badges, and sensitive callouts.
- **Neutral / Canvas (`#F7F1DE` - Pure Cream):** The warm, non-glare foundation. Replaces stark digital white with calm, organic warmth that honors long reading sessions and deep reflection.

### Atmospheric Roles
- **Default Theme:** Light mode characterized by Pure Cream backgrounds, Sage Green headlines, and muted Bronze actions.
- **Deep Immersion (Manifesto / Shadow Work container):** Full inversion using Primary Sage Green (`#748873`) as the background canvas with Pure Cream (`#F7F1DE`) editorial typography and Pale Pink (`#FFDCDC`) subdued accents.

## Typography

The typographic pairing reflects dualities: **Playfair Display** grounds the system in literary depth, contemplation, and ancient dignity, while **Montserrat** supplies an open, geometric, modern humanism that ensures complete legibility for introspective body copy and logistical therapy scheduling.

- **Display & Headlines:** Use Playfair Display with intentional, relaxed letter-spacing. Avoid aggressive uppercase treatments on headlines; title casing or natural sentence casing enhances genuine warmth.
- **Body & Prose:** Montserrat is calibrated with spacious line heights (`1.6` to `1.7`) to establish calming readability and reduce cognitive strain.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Uppercase Montserrat styled with wide letter-spacing (`0.08em` - `0.1em`) reserved strictly for categories, metadata, and form titles.

## Layout & Spacing

Space in this design system is treated as a clinical boundary and an emotional breathing room. We reject cramped layouts, sensory overload, and aggressive conversion tricks.

- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop views utilize a 12-column grid capped at a maximum width of `1280px` centered within the canvas, featuring `gutter-desktop` (40px) and wide outer page margins (`margin-desktop` / 80px). Mobile collapses to a single-column or 4-column fluid structure with 20px margins.
- **Vertical Rhythm (Generous Breathing Room):** Major content modules are partitioned by `space-2xl` (80px / `py-20`) on tablet/mobile and `space-3xl` (96px / `py-24`) on desktop. This pacing forces the user to de-escalate scrolling momentum and digest the philosophy.
- **Internal Component Paddings:** Cards, modals, and narrative blocks use generous internal offsets (`space-lg` to `space-xl`), ensuring no text ever feels pressed against borders.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids synthetic, dark drop shadows and high-gloss skeuomorphism. Depth is rooted in physical materiality—specifically layer stacking and warm, low-opacity ambient light.

- **Tonal Layering:** The primary mechanism for hierarchy is surface-container separation: Pure Cream canvas (`#F7F1DE`) hosts elevated cards crafted in pure white parchment (`#FFFFFF` with 70% opacity) or Pale Pink (`#FFDCDC` with 40% opacity).
- **Subtle Organic Shadows:** When an element must float (e.g., active dropdown, focused drawer, primary button hover), apply a warm, diffuse ambient shadow:
  - *Resting:* None or `0 2px 12px -2px rgba(116, 136, 115, 0.06)`
  - *Floating / Hover:* `0 12px 32px -6px rgba(167, 127, 96, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(116, 136, 115, 0.04)`
- **Hairline Framing:** Surface separation is reinforced using a 1px boundary line tinted in tone-on-tone Sage Green at 15% opacity (`rgba(116, 136, 115, 0.15)`) or Earthy Bronze at 20% opacity (`rgba(167, 127, 96, 0.2)`).

## Shapes

The geometry of the interface evokes organic stones, smoothed clay vessels, and hand-cut paper. 

- **Cards & Primary Modules:** Styled with `rounded-2xl` (1.5rem / 24px) to convey an embrace rather than sharp architectural barriers.
- **Inputs & Smaller Interactive Elements:** Styled with medium curvature (0.5rem - 0.75rem / 8px - 12px) to keep practical tools stable and recognizable.
- **Chips & Pills:** Formed with full circle/pill radiuses for non-threatening categorical tags.
- **Dividers:** Soft, organic hairline strokes rather than solid thick dividers, often tapered at both terminals.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Earthy Bronze):** Solid background of `#A77F60` with Pure Cream text (`#F7F1DE`), font `label-lg` uppercase. Padding `1rem 2rem`, roundedness `0.75rem`. Hover state subtle brightness reduction and ambient bronze glow.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background transparent, 1.5px border in `#748873`, text `#748873`. Hover fills with `#748873` at 8% opacity.
- **Deep Sanctuary Variant (for Manifesto container):** Pure Cream border and text on dark Sage canvas, filling with `#F7F1DE` with text transitioning to `#748873` on interaction.

### Cards
- **Contemplative Cards:** White parchment surface (`#FFFFFF`, 80% opacity), `rounded-2xl`, 1px border in `rgba(116, 136, 115, 0.12)`. Padding `2.5rem`.
- **Shadow & Light Highlight Cards:** Surface in Pale Pink (`#FFDCDC` at 50% opacity), warm bronze hairline border `rgba(167, 127, 96, 0.25)`, soft sage typography. Used for shadow integration exercises, vulnerability prompts, and intake milestones.

### Input Fields & Forms
- **Text Inputs & Textareas:** Background `#FFFFFF` at 60% opacity with Pure Cream base. Border 1px `rgba(116, 136, 115, 0.3)`. Text in `#748873`. Focus state switches border to `#A77F60` with a 2px outer ring in `rgba(167, 127, 96, 0.15)`. Labels set in `label-md` uppercase.

### Chips & Selection Pills
- **Filter & Tag Chips:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padding `0.5rem 1.25rem`. Unselected state: Pure Cream with a 1px Sage outline (`rgba(116, 136, 115, 0.2)`). Selected state: Pale Pink background (`#FFDCDC`) with Sage Green text and Earthy Bronze indicator dot.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Control Elements:** Unchecked box uses 1.5px stroke of Sage Green. Checked state fills with `#748873`, displaying a Pure Cream check glyph or center dot. Soft touch transitions (`200ms ease-in-out`).

### Reflection / Journaling Block
- An editorial component specific to therapy intake: indented left border with a 3px Earthy Bronze vertical spine, ambient Pure Cream backdrop, Playfair Display italic text with generous line height, creating a sacred space for client intention setting.
